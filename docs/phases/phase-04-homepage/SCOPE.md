# Phase 04: Homepage — Scope

## Objective
Design and implement a visually rich, responsive, and conversion-optimized homepage for BricksBazar inspired by top real estate platforms, featuring a functional multi-tab search hero, 13 tailored content sections, realistic Indian locations, clear demo badges, and direct inquiry dialogs.

## Deliverables
1. Architectural Hero Section:
   - High-contrast typography and subtle background overlay.
   - Interactive search panel with 4 category modes: Buy, Rent, Commercial, Plots.
   - Dynamic parameter routing to `/buy`, `/rent`, `/commercial`, `/plots`.
2. 13 Specialized Content Sections:
   - Section 1: Hero and search panel
   - Section 2: Explore by property type (Apartments, Villas, Independent Homes, Plots, Commercial, Penthouses)
   - Section 3: Popular metros (Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Nashik)
   - Section 4: Featured verified properties with price, rate/sq.ft, BHK specs, and contact triggers
   - Section 5: Trending localities with annual price growth metrics
   - Section 6: New and upcoming projects with RERA badges
   - Section 7: Featured developers portfolio
   - Section 8: Verified real estate agents and consultants
   - Section 9: Construction materials marketplace preview with quotation triggers
   - Section 10: Recently added property listings
   - Section 11: Real estate insights and buying guides
   - Section 12: Property listing CTA banner for owners and landlords
   - Section 13: Partner registration CTA banner for agents and suppliers
3. Interactive Contact Modal:
   - Connects directly with the consultant of any clicked property card.
