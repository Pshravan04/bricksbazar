# Phase 10: Construction Marketplace — Scope

## Objective
Implement quotation-based construction materials marketplace and supplier directories (`/marketplace`, `/marketplace/category/[slug]`, `/marketplace/product/[slug]`, `/suppliers`, `/suppliers/[slug]`). Connect builders, contractors, and home renovators with certified building material dealers for cement, steel, vitrified tiles, and paints.

## Deliverables
1. Marketplace Homepage (`/marketplace`):
   - Category grid covering 10 core construction materials.
   - Popular certified manufacturer brands (UltraTech, Tata Tiscon, Kajaria, Asian Paints).
   - Featured products catalog with quotation triggers.
   - Supplier registration banner.
2. Category Catalog (`/marketplace/category/[slug]`):
   - Categorized listings with breadcrumbs and availability.
3. Product Details View (`/marketplace/product/[slug]`):
   - High-resolution product imagery and technical specs table.
   - Authorized supplier business details.
   - Quotation request form.
4. Suppliers Directory & Profile (`/suppliers` & `/suppliers/[slug]`):
   - Regional dealer directory with categories served and phone contacts.
   - Business catalog and procurement inquiry desk.
