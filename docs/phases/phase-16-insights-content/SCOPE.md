# Phase 16: Real Estate Insights & Content — Scope

## Objective
Establish BricksBazar as the authoritative knowledge center and regulatory reference for Indian real estate by publishing in-depth market intelligence reports, RERA legal compliance checklists, and structural engineering materials handbooks. Deliver foundational institutional pages (About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, FAQ) conforming to Indian law.

## Deliverables
1. **Insights Publication Engine (`/insights`)**:
   - Categorized intelligence articles: Market Trends, RERA & Legal, Home Buying, Construction.
   - Editorial headline banner with featured report highlights.
   - Search across titles, excerpts, and author names.
   - Article cards with reading times, publication dates, and author credentials.

2. **Dynamic Article Reading Experience (`/insights/[slug]`)**:
   - Sticky desktop Table of Contents with jump links.
   - Author profile card with domain specialization.
   - Social sharing triggers (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, WhatsApp, Clipboard copy).
   - Structured typography with key takeaway callout boxes.
   - Contextual related articles recommendation grid.

3. **Institutional Pages**:
   - `/about`: Company mission, four operational pillars, and pan-India network metrics.
   - `/contact`: Interactive multi-department inquiry form, headquarters addresses in Mumbai (BKC), Bengaluru, and Pune, toll-free hotline, and email support.
   - `/privacy-policy`: Comprehensive privacy terms aligned with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and the **Information Technology Act, 2000** of India.
   - `/terms-and-conditions`: Terms of service, RERA mandatory compliance disclosures, intermediary liability limitations under Section 79 of IT Act, and Mumbai jurisdiction.
   - `/faq`: Interactive accordion FAQ covering Homebuyers, Property Owners, Real Estate Agents, and Construction Material Buyers.
