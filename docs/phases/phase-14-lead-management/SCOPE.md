# Phase 14: Lead Management & Exchange — Scope

## Objective
Equip real estate professionals (agents, brokers, developer sales desks) with a dedicated CRM pipeline and co-broking requirement marketplace. This ensures high-velocity inbound lead tracking, instant status transitions, activity logging, and standardized agent-to-agent co-broking collaboration across Indian real estate markets.

## Key Modules
1. **Lead Management CRM (`/dashboard/leads`)**:
   - High-level conversion and inquiry metrics (Total Leads, New Inquiries, Site Visits Scheduled, Deals Closed).
   - Interactive pipeline table with multi-factor filtering (Status, Lead Type, Free-text search by customer, property, phone, locality).
   - In-line pipeline status updater (New, Contacted, Qualified, Site Visit Scheduled, Negotiation, Converted, Closed, Rejected).
   - Client interaction slide-over modal with communication shortcuts (Direct Phone call, WhatsApp Web, Email), historical inquiry messages, and timestamped consultant activity notes.
   - Lead CSV export simulator with notification feedback.

2. **Lead Exchange & Co-Broking Board (`/dashboard/lead-exchange`)**:
   - Peer-to-peer requirement exchange for verified MahaRERA and State RERA licensed brokers.
   - Client requirement feed with target localities, budget parameters, urgency horizon, and standardized 50:50 commission split disclosures.
   - Automated inventory match indicators highlighting synergies between peer requirements and active portfolio listings.
   - "Connect to Co-Broke" modal displaying broker credentials, licensing records, and direct WhatsApp / Telephone channels.
   - "Post Client Requirement" modal allowing agents to broadcast verified buyer / corporate tenant requirements.
